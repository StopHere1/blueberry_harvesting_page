Harvest in action

Scan, approach, grasp, detach — the full loop running on the physical rig.

Overview

A global camera scans the bush and proposes cluster waypoints; the arm presents an in-hand camera for close-range visual servoing; a soft rolling gripper grasps and detaches while watching motor current, so it never crushes the fruit.

Perceive

Vision-guided targeting

YOLO cluster detection, an ArUco-anchored world frame, and PCA orientation estimation feed 3-D harvest waypoints.

Reach

6-DOF kinematics

Analytic-seeded numerical IK solves both position and the radial approach orientation needed for grasping.

Grasp

Force-aware rolling gripper

Current feedback clamps and detaches clusters gently — the core research contribution.

Workspace feasibility

Reaching a point is not enough — the arm must present the gripper in a usable pose. Sweeping joint space on a grid labels each voxel by its best inverse-kinematic outcome, separating the dexterous region from merely position-reachable space.

A · Dexterous

Reachable with the radial approach orientation required for grasping.

B · Position only

Reachable in position (within 5 mm) but not in the required orientation.

C · Marginal

Residual position error up to 20 mm — near the boundary of the shell.

Category D — forward-kinematically reachable but with no valid inverse solution — together with C is confined to the thin inner/outer boundaries of the annular shell, so the interior of the workspace is fully dexterous.

Current-based, force-aware grasping

Delicate fruit bruises under low force. Rather than adding a tactile sensor, the gripper infers contact force from Dynamixel motor current — taking that from binary contact detection to calibrated, regulated force control.

01 · MEASURE

Ground-truth force

Ramp closure against a force sensor; log current vs. force, up and down for hysteresis.

02 · MODEL

Current → force

Fit F = m·I + b; locate the current at a configurable bruise-force threshold.

03 · REGULATE

Hold a gentle F*

A model-based controller drives estimated clamp force to a target in Newtons.

04 · COMPARE

Bruise-risk metrics

Overshoot, settling time and time-above-bruise vs. the threshold controller.
